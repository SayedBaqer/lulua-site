LuLua Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

We collect no data

LuLua does not collect, store or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. The app does not connect to our servers.

What stays on your device

Your level progress, pearl scores and sound setting are saved only on your iPhone or iPad. Deleting the app removes them.

Purchases

LuLua Pass (the Sea world) is an auto-renewing subscription handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive or store your payment details or your Apple ID. You can manage or cancel it in iPhone Settings, your name, Subscriptions.

Language

The app follows your phone's language (Arabic or English). It only reads this setting on your device.

Children

LuLua contains no ads, no links out and no chat, and collects nothing from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
