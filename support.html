Common questions

How do I play? Swipe across a wooden part (or tap it) to snap it. The pearl rolls to Hudhud or Fuqma. Tap a perch to move them.

A level feels impossible. Every level has a solution that collects all pearls. Try snapping in a different order or waiting for the right moment, and tell us the level name if you are still stuck.

How do I restore LuLua Pass? Main menu, then "Restore purchases".

How do I cancel my subscription? iPhone Settings, your name, Subscriptions, LuLua Pass.

Which languages are supported? Arabic and English, following your phone's language.
